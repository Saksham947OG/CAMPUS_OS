## How to change a decision

Add an entry: `DEC-###`, date, requested change, current rule, proposed rule, owner/approval, affected files, tests, migration/data risk. Do not silently rewrite a `USER CONFIRMED` decision. Update the corresponding spec and acceptance tests after approval.
