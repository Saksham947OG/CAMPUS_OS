# CampusOS walkthrough: 4 minutes, built around Time Tax

Live site: https://campusos-one-mu.vercel.app/ · Reference week: Mon 2 – Sun 8 Nov 2026. Campus AI is left out on purpose.

The story in one line: **every minute lost to a queue, a full room or a missed message is a "time tax". CampusOS shows it, then removes it.**

## Before you start (30 seconds of setup, not on camera)

- Open the live site on the **Ananya Sharma** profile, light or dark theme, window at least 1280 px wide.
- Open Settings once and check "Campus data" is shown in the top bar (not a demo-only label).
- The "Campus in motion" strip is a simulation. If you mention it, call it a simulation.

## Say it honestly (the questions you will get)

- **Is this measured?** No. Ananya's total is built from reported waits on different days. Abeer's and Myra's include owner-authorised planning assumptions for processes with no supplied wait. The app labels all of it "potential reduction".
- **Is it per day?** Not claimed. Do not say "minutes saved per day". Say "potential reduction across these four processes".
- **Is anything live?** No. Occupancy, bookings, orders and requests are saved in this browser. Nothing is sent to campus staff, a café or a laundry.
- **What would make it real?** Timed before and after observations (the Observations tab) and a real backend, planned for the final phase.

## If you run short on time

Cut in this order: Academics and Administration (3:00), Library and Classrooms (2:45), Find a time (1:40). Never cut the three totals.
