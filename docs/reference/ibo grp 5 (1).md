2. **Academic calendar**  
   Display semester dates, holidays, examination periods, assignment deadlines and college events.  
   Send reminders for upcoming deadlines and notify students when dates change.  
   Filter by semester, course, batch or event type.  
   

Another section on our portal is the Academic Calendar and this one fixes a problem everyone has right now important dates are scattered all over the place. There’s a PDF calendar somewhere, exam dates come through emails, a deadline gets dropped in a WhatsApp group, and a holiday or schedule change gets announced in class and then half the batch never hears about it due to which people also ends up missing submissions and dealdines . What we’re doing is putting everything into one calendar on the portal. You open the Academic Calendar section and see your month, week, or a simple list view, whichever you prefer. Everything is colour-coded, so classes, exams, assignment deadlines, holidays, and college events each have their own colour and you can tell what’s what at a glance. The calendar is also personalised so you can filter out the  batch, section, or even by course and it only shows what’s relevant to you instead of dumping the whole college’s schedule on you. You can tap any date to see details like the subject, time, venue, and professor. The useful part is the reminders. You get notifications before things happen, like a day before a submission or a week before an exam, and you can choose how early you want them. There’s also a “coming up” strip at the top showing your next few deadlines with a countdown, so you know what’s due without scrolling through anything. If a class gets rescheduled or cancelled, the calendar updates live and you get a notification right away . You can also add your own personal events, like a group meeting or a study session, and sync the whole thing with Google Calendar so it sits alongside the rest of your life. On the admin side, faculty or the academic office get a simple panel to add or edit events, choose which batches and sections they apply to, and publish. The moment they change something, the affected students are notified automatically.

13. **Parcel and courier alerts: (**the gate or office logs a parcel, the student gets a ping, and uncollected ones are flagged.) 

             

What we’re doing is making it simple. When a parcel arrives, the gate or office staff log it on the portal by entering the student’s name or ID, the courier name, and the date. The moment it’s logged, the student gets a notification saying their parcel has arrived and where to collect it.The status of each parcel is shown on the portal as arrived, collected, or uncollected. When the student picks it up, the staff mark it as collected. Past parcels are saved in a history tab.If a parcel isn’t collected within a few days, it gets flagged. The student gets a reminder, and the staff can see a list of all uncollected parcels in one place. Everything is recorded, so there’s a clear log of what came, when it came, and who collected it.this solves the problem of theft of parcels too.

16. **Central Michigan University — Lost & Found**  
    It operates an online lost-and-found database with filters for reporting date, location, item type, and other characteristics.  
      
    A proper campus lost and found page rather than a cluttered whatsapp groupYou open the section and see two tabs: Lost and Found. If you lost something, click Report Lost Item, add a photo if you have one, pick a category (bottle, ID card, charger, books, keys, etc.), write a short description, and mention where and when you last saw it. If you found something, click Report Found Item, upload a photo, say where you found it, and where it’s kept right now, like the security desk or the office.  Everything shows up as a simple photo gallery that you can search and filter by category, date, or location. If you spot your item, you click Claim This Item and answer a quick question about it, like a detail only the owner would know. This stops people from claiming things that aren’t theirs. The finder gets notified and you both sort out the handover. The portal also tries to match things on its own. If someone reports a lost black bottle and someone else uploads a found one around the same place and time, both of them get a ping saying there might be a match. And things gets reported back to their owner they get removed from the lost and found gallery
